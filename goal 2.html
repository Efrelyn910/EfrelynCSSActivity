The internet has changed the way people learn. Students can take online courses, watch tutorial videos, or join study groups on the web. Because of this easy access to information, learning has become faster and more flexible than ever before

The internet has done something no classroom wall ever could – it erased the boundaries of where and when learning happens. From a student in rural India accessing an MIT lecture to a working professional in Lagos earning a degree online, the web has fundamentally changed who gets to learn, how they learn, and how institutions teach.
